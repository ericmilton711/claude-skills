# Meeting with Hollester's

## Workflow

1. User provides a transcript (pasted text or file path) from a meeting with Hollester's
2. Process the transcript into structured meeting minutes

## Output Format

### Meeting Minutes — Hollester's

**Date:**
**Attendees:**

#### Key Topics Discussed
- Bullet points summarizing each major topic

#### Decisions Made
- Clear statements of what was decided

#### Action Items
- [ ] Task — Owner — Due date (if mentioned)

#### Notes
- Any other relevant details, context, or follow-ups mentioned

## Instructions

- Keep language professional but concise
- Preserve names and roles mentioned in the transcript
- If the transcript is unclear on a point, flag it rather than guessing
- Group related discussion points under a single topic heading
- Prioritize action items and decisions — those are the most important outputs
